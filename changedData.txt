The message layouts change; regenerate your encoders and decoders using this schema. Single and batch place-order requests add optional rpiPxRound (RPI price rounding) and reduceOnly fields. Single and batch amend-order requests add optional rpiPxRound . In single and batch place/amend requests, expTime is renamed to expTimeUs . In all place/amend/cancel responses, ts is renamed to tsUs . These fields continue to use microseconds. Single place/amend/cancel responses add the variable-length UTF-8 error message msg . Batch place/amend/cancel responses rename the data group to results , with entries in request order (up to 20). They add the sMsgDetails group containing per-order error messages ( sMsg ), linked to results by the zero-based resultIndex , and a batch-level msg . Both msg and sMsg use varString16 , a variable-length UTF-8 string with a uint16 length prefix.\nPrivate data: okx_sbe_private_1_1.xml\nSchema id remains 1 ; version changes from 0 to 1 , and semanticVersion changes to 1.1.0 . In orders , fillTime , uTime , and cTime are renamed to fillTimeUs , uTimeUs , and cTimeUs . In fills , ts is renamed to tsUs .
